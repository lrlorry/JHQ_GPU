Four results the fronts cannot show

Recall–throughput curves say where the method lands. These say why, and each one changed what to work on next.

Recall is lost by routing, not by ranking

Split of the missing recall at alpha=100. Routing is the share of the true top-10 whose IVF list was never opened; ranking is what survived routing and was then lost by the primary filter or the refinement.

The ranking loss is 0.13% to 0.51% across three datasets and a 32× range of nprobe. Everything downstream of coarse routing — the equation-4 primary code, the ADC scan, the top-ck selection, the residual refinement — together loses at most half a point of what routing hands it. No work on the scan can raise recall.

The scan reads up to 2.2× what was reported

Measured candidates over the N×nprobe/nlist estimate every table in this project quoted before JHQ_DIAG existed.

Lists are not equal length, and the lists a query probes are not a random sample: a query lands near a dense region and the dense region's lists are the long ones. Every efficiency comparison that used the estimate flattered JHQ, worst on BGE-M3 — which is also where the gap to a graph index is widest.

The scan's parts do not scale together

Scan latency split by compiling parts out, at the default block size and at the one the fronts run. Compaction, lookup, gather and the rest.

The lookup was recorded as the largest single component — 42–52% at BLOCK=256. At 1024 it is 14–15%, and it is the part that responds most to more warps. Compaction gets more expensive in absolute time on the two large sets. Every diagnostic taken at the default block size had to be re-read.

The training set pays below 20 points a centroid and costs above it

Matched-recall QPS ratio from raising JHQ_N_TRAIN at fixed nlist. The bar is the range over the front; the dot is the median.

Below about ten points a centroid, k-means is degenerate — most centroids never leave initialisation and the partition is wildly uneven — and repairing it cuts Stella's candidates 35% at higher recall. Above twenty the quantizer is already fine and more training only sharpens its density-following, so Vogue's candidates rise 95%. Same knob, opposite directions.
