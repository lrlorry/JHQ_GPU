`../frontier.html` is the earlier report on the v39 fronts. It is **not**
superseded in the sense of being wrong: it records what those fronts said and
two harness faults found alongside them. It is left exactly as it was.

## Rebuilding

```sh
python3 scripts/make_figures.py      # fronts + report panels, from the repo root
python3 scripts/make_findings.py     # findings.html, entirely generated
```

Both read the raw logs under `results/` — nothing is hand-entered.
`make_figures.py` splices freshly drawn SVG panels into the two front pages and
leaves their prose alone; `make_findings.py` writes `findings.html` whole, from
`scripts/_findings.css`.

Earlier states of these pages were overwritten in place during the session and
were not kept. They do not need to be: every chart is derived from logs that
are committed, so any of them can be redrawn. What cannot be recovered is the
prose as it stood at each step, and the git history has that.

## What a front here is

The Pareto envelope over **every nlist and nprobe measured** for that dataset
at that Br: 620 rows across six logs. Rows where `nprobe > nlist` are dropped —
past that the probe list repeats and the row is the same measurement at a
higher price, which is what once made vogue's top look like a 3x regression.

Four nlist per dataset, and **every one of them contributes points somewhere**,
which is the nlist sweep's result restated on the chart. Fronts are 16 to 29
points.

## What the pages will not support

- **The top of the front is not interpolatable.** It is near-vertical, and
  recall varies up to 1e-3 between builds, which is worth 2-3x of apparent QPS
  there. The panel speedups are quoted at 0.85 of the overlap for that reason;
  the top band is compared instead at matched nlist and nprobe over a median of
  three builds (`results/front6/TOPBAND.md`).
- **The baselines are older measurements.** cuVS fronts come from commit
  `305e763` and a separate session. Only the JHQ rows are new.
- **One alpha.** alpha=100 throughout, matching the published sweep.
